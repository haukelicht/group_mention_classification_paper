Patterns of convergence between group mention classifications of our attribute-centered classifier and Horne et al.'s group category classifier. The figure reports normalized \emph{Pointwise Mutual Information} (nPMI) values that measure the strength of association of label classes on a scale ranging from -1 (systematic disassociation) through 0 (independence) to +1 (perfect co-occurrence).  y-axis labels indicate the attribute category in our scheme; x-axis labels the group categories in Horne et al.'s scheme. Plot panels separated by economic and non-economic attributes. \emph{Note:} heatmap columns sorted (for each attribute dimension) using hierarchical clustering to better reveal conceptual overlap.
